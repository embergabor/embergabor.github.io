GOG Disc Archiver

Back up your GOG game installers to Blu-ray and DVD, verify every file, and print matching case covers.

  • Burns installer folders straight to BD-R or DVD+R, with no disc image
  • Reads every file back after burning and compares checksums
  • Splits big games across discs and packs small ones together
  • Keeps a catalogue of your discs and re-checks old ones
  • Prints Blu-ray and jewel case covers with box art

Film Companion

Everything around scanning developed 35 mm film, from marking the roll to filing the prints in a binder.

  • Nine steps per roll, from marking the film to the archive
  • Follows SilverFast as it scans, frames appear strip by strip
  • Prints sleeve labels and A4 contact sheets
  • Writes title, date and place into the files as XMP

WaveSafe for Steam Deck

An offline music player in the Quick Access Menu. Your own files play in the background while you game.

  • Play, skip and seek over any running game
  • Gapless playback with ReplayGain
  • Its own music volume, game audio untouched
  • No streaming, no accounts, and it never changes your files

GOG Installer

A fullscreen, gamepad-first app that runs GOG installers from your server under Proton and adds the games to Steam.

  • Installs straight from the share, nothing copied to the Deck first
  • Select several games and install them one after another
  • Adds finished games to Steam with their artwork
  • Uses only what SteamOS already ships

Calm UI for KOReader

A radically simplified KOReader for Kobo e-readers: a library and a reader, with every setting you need in one sheet.

  • Two screens, no menus, status bars or hidden gestures
  • Tap the page for one sheet: light, warmth, text and page
  • Drag along the screen edges for brightness and warmth
  • A plugin and a few patches, not a fork

CrossInk Library Scan

A fork of CrossInk that adds a real library: scan the SD card once, then browse every book by author or series.

  • All Books, Authors and Series, series in reading order
  • Reads EPUB series metadata during the scan
  • Index kept on the SD card, so big libraries stay out of RAM
  • Scanned books open with no "Indexing…" pause

Simplex

A deliberately simple Android TV client for Plex. It shows your movies and shows and plays the files as they are.

  • No detail pages: OK plays, hold OK for a short menu
  • Surround sound passed through to your receiver untouched
  • Scrub with Plex's preview thumbnails while the video plays
  • Made for slow TVs: you never see an empty screen
